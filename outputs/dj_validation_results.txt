The primacy decomposition retains discrimination power under strong coupling (epsilon ~ 0.9-3.6).

With realistic confounds (survivorship + medication), monotone ordering is PRESERVED. The P statistic remains informative despite confound-induced compression of the covariance structure.

The ELSA result (P-slope = +0.0014/yr) IS informative: the effect exceeds the MDE (0.0004/yr), suggesting the P statistic has sufficient power to detect this magnitude.

Under proportional co-degradation (50D/50J) with both confounds, the mean P-slope is 0.0054/yr. The ELSA observed +0.0014/yr falls within the simulated distribution. The 'proportional co-degradation' interpretation IS defensible because the empirical slope is consistent with the null expectation under balanced D/J aging.
