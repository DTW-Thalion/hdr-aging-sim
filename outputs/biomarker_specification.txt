Z-scoring Reference
================================================================================
All z-scores computed against Wave 2 baseline cohort (age 50-55):
  z = (value - mu_ref) / sigma_ref
Log transforms: CRP = log(hscrp + 0.01), Triglycerides = log(trig + 0.01)
Functional axes reversed: higher = worse (dx_F = -z(grip_max))
