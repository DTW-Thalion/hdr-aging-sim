========================================================================
INTERPRETATION
========================================================================

The primacy ratio P does not show a statistically significant
trend with age (slope = -0.0084, p = 0.052).
Both D-degradation and J-degradation contribute proportionally.
→ Neither damage/stochastic nor hyperfunction theories alone
  dominate — both mechanisms contribute to aging in the OU framework.

Medication-naive subgroup (more biologically interpretable):
  P slope = 0.0014/yr, p = 0.4562
  → No significant primacy shift in unmedicated individuals

CAVEATS:
  1. Small sample sizes at extreme ages (80–85, 85–90) reduce power
  2. Cross-sectional design cannot distinguish cohort from age effects
  3. The 3-axis model (I, M, F) captures only a subset of the full
     regulatory network; results may differ with more axes
  4. Medication use compresses covariance structure; the medication-naive
     result is the more biologically interpretable one
  5. Selection bias: healthier individuals survive to older strata
  6. Wave 2 only — single cross-section, not longitudinal trend
