## 8. Critical Note: RMSSD/HRV Not Available

RMSSD and SDNN time-domain HRV measures are **NOT** in the standard InCHIANTI data release.
The N-axis uses **resting heart rate** (X_FC, 40-120 bpm) as a proxy.
Higher HR = worse autonomic function (sign-flipped to match positive-delta = decline convention).
This is mechanistically inferior to RMSSD but still captures parasympathetic tone directionally.
